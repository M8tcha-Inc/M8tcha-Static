## 1. Executive Summary

### Company Overview

M8tcha, Inc. is a Delaware C Corporation building a next-generation social discovery platform to help people form meaningful relationships through live conversations, communities, and real-world experiences.

The company moves beyond swipe-based dating by creating an ecosystem where users discover friendships, communities, and romantic relationships through authentic interaction. Key elements include:

- Live video social Rooms
- Community-based discovery
- Event experiences
- Relationship matching

The mobile application MVP is complete and available on the Apple App Store. Current priorities are scaling adoption, improving engagement, and expanding community features.

---

## 2. Company Overview

**Legal Structure:** Delaware C Corporation

**Industry:** Consumer Technology / Social Networking / Dating

**Stage:** Pre-Seed / Early Growth

M8tcha centers the product around interaction-first discovery to recreate social contexts where relationships develop.

## 3. Mission, Vision & Values

**Mission:** To help people create meaningful connections through shared experiences, live conversations, and communities.

**Vision:** To become the world's leading social discovery platform where people meet through authentic interactions instead of endless scrolling.

**Core Values:**

- Human Connection First - Technology should enhance relationships, not replace them.
- Authenticity - People connect better when they can express themselves beyond a profile.
- Community - Strong relationships often begin through shared interests and experiences.
- Safety - Trust and user protection are foundational to a healthy platform.

---

## 4. The Problem

Traditional dating apps have optimized for swiping and time-in-app, resulting in:

- Superficial interactions
- Low response rates and ghosting
- Difficulty determining compatibility from a profile

Social isolation persists due to moving, remote work, and limited local opportunities. People need better ways to discover communities and build relationships.

---

M8tcha operates at the intersection of:

- Online Dating
- Social Networking
- Live Video Communities
- Local Experiences and Events

Each area contributes large addressable markets and complementary user behavior.

## 6. The M8tcha Solution

M8tcha: Community → Conversation → Experience → Connection

## 13. Competitive Landscape

Competitors and positioning:

- Dating apps (Tinder, Bumble, Hinge): large audiences but profile-first and transactional.
- Social/community platforms (Discord, Meetup): strong community tools but not optimized for romantic discovery.

M8tcha combines community discovery, live interaction, and dating intent.

---

## 14. Competitive Advantages

Compared to other categories, M8tcha offers a combined set of features: profile discovery, live video interaction, communities, dating intent, and real-world events - supporting higher-quality relationship discovery.
